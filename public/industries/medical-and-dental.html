Questions

FAQ

What medical, dental and vet offices ask us most.

What about HIPAA and patient privacy?

Anything that handles patient health information needs HIPAA safeguards. A general FAQ assistant can be set up so it doesn't collect health details at all. Where patient data is involved, we use vendors that sign Business Associate Agreements and review the setup with you before building.

Can the assistant give medical advice?

No. It answers general office questions such as hours, services, insurance and preparation, and directs anything clinical or urgent to your staff or emergency services.

Does it connect to our practice software?

It depends on the system and what access it allows. We check this in discovery and only propose connections that can be done securely.

What does it cost?

A patient FAQ assistant usually falls in our AI Chatbot range of $5,000 to $15,000; intake automation projects start around $5,000. Each is quoted at a fixed price.
