Questions

FAQ

What contractors and home service companies ask us most.

We already use Jobber / ServiceTitan. Why do we need more?

Those tools run your jobs well. We help when you need numbers they don't show, data combined with QuickBooks, or a workflow they don't cover. We'll tell you if your current software can already do it.

How accurate can job costing be?

As accurate as the time, materials and invoice data behind it. Part of discovery is finding gaps, like unassigned labor, and fixing how they're recorded.

Will my field crews need training?

Anything they touch works on a phone and is kept simple. Most of the reporting work happens behind the scenes for the office and owners.

What does a first project cost?

A job-costing dashboard usually falls in our Dashboard Starter range of $3,000 to $8,000, quoted at a fixed price after a short paid discovery.
