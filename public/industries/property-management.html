Questions

FAQ

What property managers ask us most.

Will the chatbot give tenants wrong answers?

It answers only from documents you approve, is tested on real tenant questions, and hands off to your staff when it isn't sure or the issue is urgent.

Can it handle maintenance requests?

Yes. It can collect the details and photos and pass them to your maintenance process or software, with emergencies flagged to a person right away.

Does it work with AppFolio, Buildium or Yardi?

It works alongside them. Deeper connections depend on what access each system allows; we confirm what's possible for your setup in discovery.

What does it cost?

A focused tenant and owner chatbot usually falls in our AI Chatbot range of $5,000 to $15,000, plus modest monthly AI usage costs we estimate up front.
