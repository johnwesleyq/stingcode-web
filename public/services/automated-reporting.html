Questions

FAQ

Straight answers to what people ask us most about Automated reporting.

Why not just use Zapier ourselves?

For simple tasks you should. We step in when workflows need logic, error handling, larger volumes or systems Zapier doesn't connect to, and we will tell you if an off-the-shelf tool is enough.

What happens if one of our apps changes?

Automations include error alerts, so a problem is noticed right away. A care plan covers fixing them when vendors change things.

Do we have to switch software?

No. We automate around the tools you already use and only suggest a change when it clearly pays off.

How do you handle passwords and access?

We use the access methods each app provides, with accounts owned by you, and you can revoke our access at any time.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most clients start with a single workflow, see the time saved, then decide on the next one.
