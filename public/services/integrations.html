Questions

FAQ

Straight answers to what people ask us most about Integrations & APIs.

Is there an off-the-shelf connector for this?

Sometimes. We check first and recommend one if it handles your rules. Custom integrations make sense when the ready-made ones are close but not right.

What if the integration breaks?

Every integration logs its work and alerts us or you when something fails. A care plan covers fixes when vendors change their systems.

Can data sync both ways?

Yes, but two-way sync needs clear rules about which system wins when records conflict. We define those with you up front.

Is our data secure?

Connections use each vendor's official access methods with accounts you own, encrypted in transit, and with only the permissions needed.

Our system has no API. Can you still connect it?

Often, yes, through scheduled exports, direct database access or files. We assess it in discovery.
