Questions

FAQ

Straight answers to what people ask us most about AI chatbots & assistants.

Will the chatbot make things up?

We ground answers in your own documents, test on real questions before launch, and set it to hand off to a person when it isn't sure. No AI is perfect, so we design for safe failure.

Is our data used to train AI models?

We build on business AI services, review their data terms with you, and keep your documents in accounts you own. We explain exactly where your data goes before anything is built.

What are the ongoing costs?

Besides hosting, AI providers charge per use. For most small businesses that is modest; we estimate it in discovery based on your expected volume.

Can it connect to our systems?

Yes. Assistants can look up orders, create CRM leads, book appointments or file tickets, with permissions you control.

How do we keep answers up to date?

You update the source documents and the assistant follows. A care plan can cover regular tuning and new content.
