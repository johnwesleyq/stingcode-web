Questions

FAQ

Straight answers to what people ask us most about Dashboards & Power BI.

Power BI or Looker Studio: which should we use?

If your team already uses Microsoft 365, Power BI usually fits best and handles complex data well. Looker Studio is free to view and works nicely with Google Workspace. We compare both for your situation during discovery and recommend one.

Do we need to buy Power BI licenses?

Often, yes: people who view shared Power BI reports typically need a Microsoft license. We confirm exactly what you need, and the cost, before you commit, and suggest Looker Studio if licensing doesn't make sense.

Our data is messy. Is that a problem?

That is normal. Mapping and cleaning your data is part of discovery and the build, and we flag anything that needs a process change so the numbers stay right.

How fast will we see something?

Most clients see a first working screen within about two weeks, then refine it in short cycles with a demo each time.

Who maintains the dashboard after launch?

You own everything. We train your team and leave a written guide, and an optional care plan covers new reports, fixes and changes.
